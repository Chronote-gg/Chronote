## What Chronote does

Chronote is a Discord bot that records voice meetings, produces transcripts and notes, and keeps them in a searchable library. It can also answer questions about past meetings, speak in voice channels, and export notes to connected services such as Notion.

## Recording responsibilities

This is the part that matters most, so it is stated plainly.

**You are responsible for the legality of recordings made in your server.** Laws about recording conversations vary widely by country and by state, and some require the consent of every person being recorded. Chronote gives you the tools to record; it does not determine whether you are allowed to.

If you enable recording in a server, you agree that you will:

- Tell your members that meetings can be recorded, and make sure that is discoverable, for example in your server rules.
- Obtain whatever consent the law requires in the jurisdictions your members are in.
- Configure auto-record only in channels where recording is appropriate.
- Handle requests from your own members about recordings they appear in.

Chronote helps by being visibly present in the voice channel while it records and by posting in the channel when a meeting starts, but that is a courtesy, not a substitute for your own notice and consent practices.

## Availability

Chronote is provided as-is. We work to keep it running, and we watch it closely, but we do not promise uninterrupted service, perfect transcription accuracy, or that a given meeting will record successfully.

Transcripts and notes are produced by automated speech recognition and language models. They can contain errors, including misattributed speech. Do not treat them as a verbatim legal record without checking them. The correction and approval flow exists so a human can put the record right.
